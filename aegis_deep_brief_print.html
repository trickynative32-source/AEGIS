1 Executive Summary & Problem Alignment

AEGIS is an agentic, Windows-native desktop personal assistant engineered to fulfill Problem Statement SIH26204 (Assistive Technologies and Inclusive Innovation). It unites conversational intelligence, local computer vision, OS-level automation, low-latency audio with speech interruption (barge-in), and deep accessibility features into a unified, zero-latency desktop assistant.

Traditional voice assistants suffer from three critical flaws: (1) they lack grounding in native Windows system state, frequently hallucinating the clock and date; (2) they are cloud-dependent and blind to the physical desk environment; and (3) their media/task execution is passive, opening generic search pages that require manual clicks. AEGIS resolves all three challenges with local neural detectors, deterministic OS dispatch, and direct task resolution.

5 Hands-Free Speech & Autonomous Desktop Workflows

A. Voice Interaction with Instant Speech Barge-In

AEGIS implements hands-free audio using Faster-Whisper and Edge-TTS. When the user starts speaking while AEGIS is speaking, the frontend detects voice activity and immediately fires a barge_in packet. The backend cancels speech audio synthesis within 50ms and transitions instantly to LISTENING mode.

B. Direct YouTube Video & Music Playback

Traditional voice assistants open generic search pages requiring manual selection. AEGIS scrapes the top video result via regex matching /watch?v=([a-zA-Z0-9_-]{11}), opens the video directly in the browser, and renders an animated holographic player card in chat complete with HD thumbnail and equalizer bars.

C. Smart Flight Booking Engine

When a user requests flight booking (e.g. "Book a flight from Mumbai to Delhi this Friday"), flight_tools.py automatically parses origin, destination, and dates. If the platform is unspecified, AEGIS prompts the user with supported providers (Google Flights, Skyscanner, MakeMyTrip, Expedia, Kayak) and deep-links directly into the prefilled booking checkout page.
